# AI-Tutoring-Project
An ongoing plan to give free education through AI
- This project started in Nova High School using certain LLMs to help students. Many prompts were made overtime in order to improve and every student had their own AI chatbot custom to their needs.
- The ultimate goal was to improve education for free without the need of a physical tutor, all students chosen raised their average grade in their classes by at least 30%.
- I continue to show teachers and students how to properly use AI and have taught courses and such educating those in need.
